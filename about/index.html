About Us

Your window into Asia's fast-moving technology landscape

AsiaTechFeed is an independent technology news publication covering the most important stories across Asia's dynamic tech ecosystem — from Silicon Valley-rivaling startups in Southeast Asia to groundbreaking innovation in China, Japan, South Korea, and India.

Our Mission

To deliver timely, accurate, and insightful technology news that keeps readers ahead of the curve in one of the world's most exciting and fastest-growing tech regions.

What We Cover

We track the stories that matter across the full spectrum of Asia's tech industry:

Startups & Funding New ventures, VC rounds, and the founders shaping tomorrow.
AI & Deep Tech Artificial intelligence, robotics, semiconductors, and emerging technologies.
Big Tech Moves by Alibaba, Tencent, Samsung, SoftBank, and regional giants.
Policy & Regulation Government decisions and regulatory shifts affecting the tech sector.
E-Commerce & Fintech Digital payments, super-apps, and the future of commerce.
Markets & IPOs Tech listings, valuations, and capital market activity across the region.

Why AsiaTechFeed?

Asia is home to over half the world's internet users and some of the most ambitious technology companies on the planet. Yet comprehensive, English-language coverage of the region's full breadth remains sparse. AsiaTechFeed exists to close that gap.

We curate and deliver the news that matters — cutting through noise to give you a clear, focused feed of Asia's most significant technology developments, every day.

Our Editorial Approach

We are committed to accuracy, clarity, and editorial independence. We do not accept payment for coverage. Our reporting is driven by newsworthiness, not advertising relationships. When we make errors, we correct them promptly and transparently.
